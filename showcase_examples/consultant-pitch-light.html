The problem

Consultants lose track of relationships — quietly, expensively.

  • 01
    Scattered capture Notebooks. Notes app. WhatsApp threads. Three different CRMs that no one keeps up to date.
  • 02
    The mental ledger fails After twenty meetings in a week, "what did we last talk about" turns into improvisation.
  • 03
    Follow-ups drift "I'll send that next Friday" becomes three months later — or never.
  • 04
    Birthdays missed, renewals skipped The small touches that make a relationship feel cared for fall through the cracks first.
The cost

A consultant's book is built on memory. When memory fails, the book quietly leaks.

Most CRMs make this worse — they add an input tax instead of reducing one.

Disposition · Private by design

A tool that is yours — not the platform's.

What it is

  • Local-only SQLite database on your Mac
  • No cloud sync, no telemetry, no auth bureaucracy
  • One database per laptop · your data, your file
  • Source-of-truth for memory, prep, and follow-through
  • Built to survive PDF export, screenshots, printing

What it is not

  • Not a tool that sends client-facing messages
  • Not a WhatsApp / SMS / email broadcast drafter
  • Not a system of record · official platforms still own that
  • Not compliance-approved · not making that claim
  • Not a sales pipeline tracker · this is about relationships

A private operating aid. Quiet, considered, kept inside your laptop where it belongs.

Private aid · not a CRM, not a broadcast tool
