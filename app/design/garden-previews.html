GPT image-gen prompt (copy-paste into ChatGPT / GPT-Image) 
Generate a set of 7 minimalist black linework tattoo flower and plant elements, arranged on a clean white background with 60px of whitespace between each. Each element must be drawable in 5-20 line segments — no shading, no fill, no color, no halftones. Pure black single-weight ink line art (~1.5pt stroke) on pure white.

Elements needed (label each):
1. "tiny-bud" — a single small closed bud at the tip of a 6mm stem (height ~15mm total)
2. "sprig" — a slender stem with 3 small clustered petals at top, asymmetric (height ~22mm)
3. "daisy" — a 6-petal wildflower with a small center dot, petals oval-shaped, NOT perfectly symmetric (width ~16mm)
4. "leaf-pair" — two slender leaves on a thin stem, one slightly larger than the other (height ~20mm)
5. "rosette" — a layered wildflower with ~8 visible petals at slightly varied angles, intentional negative space around the center (width ~22mm)
6. "tendril" — a single thin curling line with a tiny coil at the tip (height ~14mm)
7. "berries" — a cluster of 4-5 small filled circles with a small leaf, like bramble berries (width ~14mm)

Style references: Hannah Snowdon micro-tattoos, single-needle botanical line tattoos, Pinterest "minimalist linework flower tattoo". Each element should feel hand-drawn (not perfectly symmetric, slight line weight variation OK) but use a single consistent black stroke color.

Output: one PNG with all 7 elements clearly separated and labeled. Each element must be self-contained for easy extraction.
